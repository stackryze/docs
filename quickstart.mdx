This quickstart walks you from signing in at Stackryze DNS to an active zone serving DNS records on PowerDNS.

## Prerequisites

Before you begin, make sure you have:

- A Stackryze account via [auth.stackryze.com](https://auth.stackryze.com)
- Either a custom domain you own at an external registrar, or a subdomain registered on [domain.stackryze.com](https://domain.stackryze.com)

## Get started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in to Stackryze DNS">
    Open [dns.stackryze.com](https://dns.stackryze.com) and sign in with **Stackryze SSO**. Your default account quota supports up to **3 zones** and **200 records per zone**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your zone">
    Click **Add Zone** and enter your domain name (for example `example.com`, or a subdomain such as `myproject.indevs.in`).

    Stackryze provisions a `NATIVE` zone on our Master/Slave PowerDNS servers with our authoritative SOA record (`ns1.stackryze.com. hostmaster.stackryze.com.`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify ownership (Stackryze subdomains only)">
    If you added a subdomain ending in `.indevs.in`, `.sryze.cc`, `.ryzedns.org`, `.nx.kg`, or `.ryzn.pro`, the zone starts in `pending_ownership` status and shows a one-time `sryze-verify-...` code:

    1. Open [domain.stackryze.com](https://domain.stackryze.com) → **My Domains** → select your subdomain.
    2. Under **DNS Ownership Verification**, click **Add Code** and paste the `sryze-verify-...` code.
    3. Return to [dns.stackryze.com](https://dns.stackryze.com) and click **Verify Ownership**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Delegate nameservers">
    At your registrar (or in your Stackryze Domains **Edit Nameservers** panel), point the domain to all three Stackryze nameservers:

    ```text
    ns1.stackryze.com
    ns2.stackryze.com
    ns3.stackryze.com
    ```

    If you created the subdomain on [domain.stackryze.com](https://domain.stackryze.com), these three nameservers are assigned by default.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify nameservers and add records">
    Click **Verify** in the Stackryze DNS dashboard to run an uncached NS check against Google DNS (`8.8.8.8`) and public resolvers. Once verified, the zone status switches to `active`.

    Add `A`, `AAAA`, `CNAME`, `MX`, `TXT`, `SRV`, or `CAA` records with TTL values between `3600` seconds (1 hour) and `604800` seconds (1 week).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm live resolution">
    Query the nameserver directly to confirm answers are live:

    ```bash
    dig A example.com @ns1.stackryze.com +short
    ```
  </Step>
</Steps>
