This guide walks you through signing in, completing GitHub verification, and claiming a free public subdomain on [domain.stackryze.com](https://domain.stackryze.com).

<Note>
  Stackryze Domains supports five extensions: `indevs.in`, `sryze.cc`, `ryzedns.org`, `nx.kg`, and `ryzn.pro`. You get **1 free slot per extension** (5 domains total) by default.
</Note>

## Step 1 — Sign in with Stackryze SSO

1. Open [domain.stackryze.com](https://domain.stackryze.com) and click **Get Started**.
2. Click **Continue with Stackryze SSO** to sign in or create an account via [auth.stackryze.com](https://auth.stackryze.com).
3. If your sign-in email ends in `@users.noreply.github.com`, you will be prompted to set and verify a deliverable personal email address so you can receive expiry warnings and account notices.

## Step 2 — Complete GitHub verification

GitHub Star KYC is required once per account before registering subdomains on any extension:

1. Open [github.com/stackryze/FreeDomains](https://github.com/stackryze/FreeDomains) and click **Star**.
2. In the dashboard, open **Register Domain** (or **My Domains**), click **Verify with GitHub**, and authorize the check.

Requirements checked automatically:

- Your GitHub account is at least **30 days old**
- That GitHub account ID is not already linked to a different Stackryze account
- Your GitHub account has starred `stackryze/FreeDomains`

## Step 3 — Choose and register your domain

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick a name and extension">
    Open **Register Domain** from the sidebar, type your desired subdomain label, and select an extension (`.indevs.in`, `.sryze.cc`, `.ryzedns.org`, `.nx.kg`, or `.ryzn.pro`).

    Naming rules:
    - **Length:** 3–63 characters
    - **Allowed characters:** lowercase `a–z`, `0–9`, and `-` (no leading/trailing hyphens, underscores, Punycode `xn--`, or reserved words)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm availability">
    The availability checker runs automatically via `GET /subdomains/check/:name`. Wait for the green checkmark indicating the label is free and not in a 7-day cooling-off period.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept terms and complete Turnstile">
    Check the [Terms of Service](https://domain.stackryze.com/terms) and [Privacy Policy](https://domain.stackryze.com/privacy) box and pass the Cloudflare Turnstile verification check.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Register Domain">
    Stackryze writes `NS` records for your subdomain in PowerDNS immediately (defaulting to `ns1.stackryze.com`, `ns2.stackryze.com`, `ns3.stackryze.com`), marks your domain `Active`, and sets expiration to **1 year** from today.
  </Step>
</Steps>
