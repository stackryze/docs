Manage nameserver delegation, renewals, DNS ownership codes, and deletions from **My Domains** at [domain.stackryze.com](https://domain.stackryze.com).

## Viewing domain details

Open **My Domains** and click **Manage** next to any registered subdomain to see:

- **Status** (`Active`, `Expired`, `Pending Deletion`, or `Suspended`)
- **Registered On** and **Expires On** dates (1-year term)
- **DNS Configuration** (`NS1`–`NS6`)
- **DNS Ownership Verification** (`dns.stackryze.com` link status)

## Updating nameservers

Every Stackryze Domain supports between **2 and 6 nameservers**. New domains start with `ns1.stackryze.com`, `ns2.stackryze.com`, and `ns3.stackryze.com`, and you can replace or edit them at any time.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Prepare your DNS provider">
    Create the zone for your full subdomain (for example `myproject.indevs.in`) at your chosen DNS provider first and note the nameserver hostnames it assigns. See [DNS providers](/guides/domains/dns-providers).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit Nameservers">
    Open **My Domains → Manage → DNS Configuration** and click **Edit Nameservers**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter 2 to 6 nameserver hostnames">
    Provide at least `NS1` and `NS2` (and up to `NS6`). Hostnames must be valid public DNS names without `http://`, IP addresses, or duplicates.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save changes">
    Click **Save Nameservers**. Stackryze updates the `NS` RRset in PowerDNS immediately (`TTL 3600`). Allow 5–10 minutes for major resolvers and up to 48 hours for full global cache propagation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Verify live parent delegation with:

```bash
dig NS yourname.indevs.in @ns1.stackryze.com +short
```

## Renewing domains

Domains are valid for **1 year** and renewals are free.

- **Renewal window:** Opens **60 days before expiry** and remains open through the **15-day post-expiry grace period**.
- **Reminder emails:** Sent at **60 days** and **10 days** prior to expiry.
- **Grace period:** On expiry, status moves from `Active` to `Expired`, while PowerDNS delegation stays active for 15 days. Renewing during grace restores status to `Active`.
- **Auto-soft-delete:** After more than 15 days past expiry without renewal, the daily job removes PowerDNS delegation, decrements your quota, and starts a **7-day cooling-off period** before permanent deletion.

To renew, open **My Domains** (or the domain's detail page) and click **Renew**.

## Requesting deletion

If you no longer need a domain:

1. Open **My Domains → Manage** and scroll to **Danger Zone**.
2. Click **Delete Domain** and confirm.

Submitting deletion moves the domain to **`Pending Deletion`** for administrator review. The domain keeps resolving until an administrator approves the deletion; once deleted, the name enters a **7-day cooling-off period** before anyone can register it again.

<Warning>
  Domains that are `Suspended` or `Pending Deletion` cannot be modified or renewed.
</Warning>

## Activity history and WHOIS privacy

- **Activity History:** View account logins, registrations, nameserver updates, renewals, and deletion requests under **Activity History**.
- **WHOIS Privacy:** Registrant email is hidden in public lookups at [domain.stackryze.com/whois](https://domain.stackryze.com/whois) by default. Toggle email visibility in **Settings → WHOIS Privacy**; name, postal address, and phone remain redacted at all times.
