Quick answers to the most common questions about [domain.stackryze.com](https://domain.stackryze.com).

## Extensions and quotas

### What extensions are available?

Stackryze Domains offers five public extensions:

| Extension | Default limit per account |
| --- | --- |
| `.indevs.in` | 1 |
| `.sryze.cc` | 1 |
| `.ryzedns.org` | 1 |
| `.nx.kg` | 1 |
| `.ryzn.pro` | 1 |

You can request quota increases by contacting [support@stackryze.com](mailto:support@stackryze.com) or joining our [Discord](https://discord.gg/wr7s97cfM7).

### Is Stackryze Domains free?

Yes. Registration and annual renewals are completely free.

### Can I rename a subdomain or move it to another extension?

No. Both the label and extension are fixed after registration.

## Verification and accounts

### Why do I need GitHub verification?

GitHub Star KYC confirms each account belongs to a real developer account at least **30 days old** before claiming subdomains on any of the five extensions. Only one Stackryze account can link a given GitHub ID.

### How do I change my email or password?

Passwords, Passkeys, and 2FA live on **Stackryze SSO** at [auth.stackryze.com](https://auth.stackryze.com). If your account signed up with a GitHub `@users.noreply.github.com` email, you can update it directly in **Settings** (or during onboarding).

## DNS and nameservers

### Can I use Cloudflare, Route 53, or my own DNS server?

Yes. Open **My Domains → Manage → DNS Configuration**, click **Edit**, and enter between 2 and 6 authoritative nameservers (`NS1`–`NS6`). See our [DNS providers](/guides/domains/dns-providers) guides.

### How do I manage DNS records directly on `dns.stackryze.com`?

Add your subdomain zone on [dns.stackryze.com](https://dns.stackryze.com), copy the `sryze-verify-...` code into **DNS Ownership Verification** on your domain's page in [domain.stackryze.com](https://domain.stackryze.com), and click **Verify Ownership**.

### Why isn't my domain resolving yet?

Remember that registering on `domain.stackryze.com` only writes the parent `NS` delegation (`TTL 3600`). You must also create your `A`, `AAAA`, or `CNAME` records inside your DNS provider (or on `dns.stackryze.com` after ownership verification), and allow up to 48 hours for global DNS cache propagation.

## Renewals and lifecycle

### When can I renew my domain?

The free renewal window opens **60 days before expiry** and remains open through the **15-day post-expiry grace period**. We send email reminders at 60 days and 10 days before expiry.

### What happens if I miss the 15-day grace period?

Domains expired for more than 15 days are automatically soft-deleted: PowerDNS delegation is removed, your quota count is freed, and the name enters a **7-day cooling-off period** before permanent purge and public release.
