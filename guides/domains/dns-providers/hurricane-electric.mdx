Use Hurricane Electric (`dns.he.net`) for free authoritative DNS hosting with IPv6 and slave zone support.

## Prerequisites

- A registered subdomain on [domain.stackryze.com](https://domain.stackryze.com) (see [Getting started](/guides/domains/getting-started))
- A free account at [dns.he.net](https://dns.he.net)

## Setup steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add your subdomain in HE.net">
    Sign in at [dns.he.net](https://dns.he.net), click **Add a new domain**, and enter your full Stackryze subdomain (for example `yourname.indevs.in`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy your assigned HE.net nameservers">
    Open the zone and copy the assigned `ns*.he.net` nameserver hostnames (HE.net provides up to 5 servers such as `ns2.he.net` through `ns5.he.net`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Delegate your subdomain in Stackryze Domains">
    Open [domain.stackryze.com](https://domain.stackryze.com) → **My Domains** → **Manage** on your domain. Under **DNS Configuration**, click **Edit**, enter your HE.net nameservers (`NS1`–`NS5`), and click **Save Nameservers**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify propagation">
    ```bash
    dig NS yourname.indevs.in @8.8.8.8 +short
    ```
  </Step>
</Steps>
