Use Hetzner DNS for free, API-accessible authoritative DNS hosting for your Stackryze subdomain.

## Prerequisites

- A registered subdomain on [domain.stackryze.com](https://domain.stackryze.com) (see [Getting started](/guides/domains/getting-started))
- A [Hetzner](https://www.hetzner.com) account

## Setup steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a zone in Hetzner DNS Console">
    Open the Hetzner DNS Console, click **Add new zone**, and enter your full Stackryze subdomain (for example `yourname.indevs.in`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy your Hetzner nameservers">
    Copy the three nameserver hostnames shown for the zone (for example `hydrogen.ns.hetzner.com`, `oxygen.ns.hetzner.com`, `helium.ns.hetzner.de`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Delegate your subdomain in Stackryze Domains">
    Open [domain.stackryze.com](https://domain.stackryze.com) → **My Domains** → **Manage** on your domain. Under **DNS Configuration**, click **Edit**, replace `NS1`–`NS3` with your Hetzner nameservers, and click **Save Nameservers**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify delegation">
    ```bash
    dig NS yourname.indevs.in @ns1.stackryze.com +short
    ```
  </Step>
</Steps>
