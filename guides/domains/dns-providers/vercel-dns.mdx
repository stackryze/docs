Use Vercel DNS for automatic edge TLS and deployment routing on your Stackryze subdomain.

## Prerequisites

- A registered subdomain on [domain.stackryze.com](https://domain.stackryze.com) (see [Getting started](/guides/domains/getting-started))
- A [Vercel](https://vercel.com) account and project

## Setup steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add your subdomain in Vercel">
    Open your Vercel project → **Settings → Domains**, enter your full Stackryze subdomain (for example `yourname.indevs.in`), and choose the **Nameservers** setup method when prompted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy your Vercel nameservers">
    Note the nameservers shown by Vercel (typically `ns1.vercel-dns.com` and `ns2.vercel-dns.com`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Delegate your subdomain in Stackryze Domains">
    Open [domain.stackryze.com](https://domain.stackryze.com) → **My Domains** → **Manage** on your domain. Under **DNS Configuration**, click **Edit**, replace **NS1** and **NS2** (removing optional `NS3` if unused) with `ns1.vercel-dns.com` and `ns2.vercel-dns.com`, and click **Save Nameservers**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify configuration in Vercel">
    Return to Vercel and refresh domain status. Allow 5–10 minutes for nameserver propagation and automatic SSL provisioning.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If you prefer keeping Stackryze DNS or Cloudflare in front, point only a `CNAME` or `A` record as instructed in Vercel instead of delegating root nameservers.
</Tip>
