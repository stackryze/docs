Use ClouDNS free DNS hosting as the authoritative nameservers for your Stackryze subdomain.

## Prerequisites

- A registered subdomain on [domain.stackryze.com](https://domain.stackryze.com) (see [Getting started](/guides/domains/getting-started))
- A free [ClouDNS](https://www.cloudns.net) account

## Setup steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a Master Zone in ClouDNS">
    Sign in to ClouDNS, click **Create Zone → Master Zone**, and enter your full subdomain (for example `yourname.indevs.in`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy your ClouDNS nameservers">
    Copy the authoritative nameserver hostnames shown for your zone (at least 2 and up to 6).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Delegate your subdomain in Stackryze Domains">
    Open [domain.stackryze.com](https://domain.stackryze.com) → **My Domains** → **Manage** on your domain. Under **DNS Configuration**, click **Edit**, enter your ClouDNS nameservers in **NS1**, **NS2** (and **+ Add nameserver** if ClouDNS provides additional servers), then click **Save Nameservers**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify propagation">
    ```bash
    dig NS yourname.indevs.in @8.8.8.8 +short
    ```
  </Step>
</Steps>
