Use Microsoft Azure DNS as the authoritative nameservers for your Stackryze subdomain.

## Prerequisites

- A registered subdomain on [domain.stackryze.com](https://domain.stackryze.com) (see [Getting started](/guides/domains/getting-started))
- An active Microsoft Azure subscription

## Setup steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a DNS zone in Azure">
    Open the [Azure Portal](https://portal.azure.com), search for **DNS zones**, and click **Create**. Enter your full Stackryze subdomain (for example `yourname.indevs.in`) as the zone name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the four Azure nameservers">
    Open the newly created DNS zone's **Overview** pane and copy the assigned `Name server 1` through `Name server 4` hostnames (for example `ns1-01.azure-dns.com` through `ns4-01.azure-dns.info`), omitting any trailing dots.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Delegate your subdomain in Stackryze Domains">
    Open [domain.stackryze.com](https://domain.stackryze.com) → **My Domains** → **Manage** on your domain. Under **DNS Configuration**, click **Edit**, replace `NS1`–`NS3` and add `NS4` with your Azure nameservers, then click **Save Nameservers**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add records and verify propagation">
    Create your `A`, `AAAA`, `CNAME`, or `TXT` record sets inside Azure DNS and verify delegation:

    ```bash
    dig NS yourname.indevs.in @ns1.stackryze.com +short
    ```
  </Step>
</Steps>
