Use Namecheap FreeDNS as the authoritative DNS host for your Stackryze subdomain.

## Prerequisites

- A registered subdomain on [domain.stackryze.com](https://domain.stackryze.com) (see [Getting started](/guides/domains/getting-started))
- A [Namecheap](https://www.namecheap.com/domains/freedns/) account

## Setup steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add your subdomain to Namecheap FreeDNS">
    Sign in to Namecheap, open **FreeDNS**, enter your full Stackryze subdomain (for example `yourname.indevs.in`), and click **Get DNS**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the FreeDNS nameservers">
    Copy the assigned nameserver hostnames (typically `freedns1.registrar-servers.com` through `freedns5.registrar-servers.com`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Delegate your subdomain in Stackryze Domains">
    Open [domain.stackryze.com](https://domain.stackryze.com) → **My Domains** → **Manage** on your domain. Under **DNS Configuration**, click **Edit**, enter the Namecheap FreeDNS hostnames (at least `NS1` and `NS2`, up to `NS6`), and click **Save Nameservers**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify propagation">
    ```bash
    dig NS yourname.indevs.in @ns1.stackryze.com +short
    ```
  </Step>
</Steps>
