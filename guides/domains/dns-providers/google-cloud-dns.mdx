Use Google Cloud DNS as the authoritative nameservers for your Stackryze subdomain.

## Prerequisites

- A registered subdomain on [domain.stackryze.com](https://domain.stackryze.com) (see [Getting started](/guides/domains/getting-started))
- A Google Cloud project with the **Cloud DNS API** enabled

## Setup steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a public managed zone">
    In **Google Cloud Console → Network Services → Cloud DNS**, click **Create Zone**, choose **Public**, and set **DNS name** to your full Stackryze subdomain (for example `yourname.indevs.in`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the four Cloud DNS nameservers">
    Open your zone's **NS** record and copy the four assigned hostnames (such as `ns-cloud-a1.googledomains.com` through `ns-cloud-a4.googledomains.com`), without trailing dots.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Delegate your subdomain in Stackryze Domains">
    Open [domain.stackryze.com](https://domain.stackryze.com) → **My Domains** → **Manage** on your domain. Under **DNS Configuration**, click **Edit**, replace `NS1`–`NS3` and add `NS4` with your Cloud DNS nameservers, then click **Save Nameservers**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify propagation">
    ```bash
    dig NS yourname.indevs.in @8.8.8.8 +short
    ```
  </Step>
</Steps>
