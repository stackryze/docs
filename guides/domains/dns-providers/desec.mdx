Use deSEC (`desec.io`) for free, privacy-focused authoritative DNS hosting for your Stackryze subdomain.

## Prerequisites

- A registered subdomain on [domain.stackryze.com](https://domain.stackryze.com) (see [Getting started](/guides/domains/getting-started))
- A free [deSEC](https://desec.io) account

## Setup steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your domain in deSEC">
    Sign in at [desec.io](https://desec.io), open **Domain Management**, click **+**, and enter your full Stackryze subdomain (for example `yourname.indevs.in`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the deSEC nameservers">
    Note the nameservers displayed by deSEC (`ns1.desec.io` and `ns2.desec.org`, or the exact servers shown for your zone).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Delegate your subdomain in Stackryze Domains">
    Open [domain.stackryze.com](https://domain.stackryze.com) → **My Domains** → **Manage** on your domain. Under **DNS Configuration**, click **Edit**, replace `NS1` and `NS2` (removing optional `NS3` if unused) with your deSEC nameservers, and click **Save Nameservers**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify delegation">
    ```bash
    dig NS yourname.indevs.in @ns1.stackryze.com +short
    ```
  </Step>
</Steps>
