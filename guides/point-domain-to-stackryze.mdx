title: "Point domain to Stackryze"
description: "Ways to connect an existing domain or Stackryze subdomain to Stackryze DNS."

---

Choose how you want to connect your domain to Stackryze DNS depending on whether it is a custom registrar domain or a Stackryze subdomain.

## Option A — Full delegation (custom domain)

Change your registrar's nameservers to `ns1.stackryze.com`, `ns2.stackryze.com`, and `ns3.stackryze.com`.

- **Best for:** domains registered at external registrars where you want Stackryze DNS to serve the entire zone (`A`, `AAAA`, `CNAME`, `MX`, `TXT`, `SRV`, `CAA`, DNSSEC, API automation).
- **Trade-off:** DNS records are managed at [dns.stackryze.com](https://dns.stackryze.com) instead of your registrar's DNS panel.
- **Walkthrough:** see [Add a custom domain](/guides/add-a-custom-domain).

## Option B — Stackryze subdomain delegation

Keep registration on [domain.stackryze.com](https://domain.stackryze.com) and either:

- Point records on [dns.stackryze.com](https://dns.stackryze.com) after completing the one-time `sryze-verify-...` ownership code handshake, **or**
- Replace `ns1–ns3.stackryze.com` in **Edit Nameservers** with any external DNS provider (see [DNS provider guides](/guides/domains/dns-providers)).

## Option C — Subdomain CNAME delegation from another DNS host

If you only want a specific hostname (such as `app.example.com`) to resolve to an endpoint managed elsewhere while keeping your root domain at your current DNS host, create a `CNAME` record at your existing DNS provider instead of transferring nameservers.
