This guide walks you through adding a custom domain from any external registrar (Namecheap, Porkbun, Cloudflare Registrar, GoDaddy, etc.) to [dns.stackryze.com](https://dns.stackryze.com).

## Prerequisites

- A Stackryze account via [auth.stackryze.com](https://auth.stackryze.com)
- Access to change nameservers at your domain registrar

<Note>
  If you want to host a Stackryze subdomain (`.indevs.in`, `.sryze.cc`, `.ryzedns.org`, `.nx.kg`, `.ryzn.pro`), follow the ownership handshake in [Nameserver delegation](/docs/domains/nameserver-delegation#using-stackryze-dns-dnsstackryzecom) after adding the zone.
</Note>

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in to Stackryze DNS">
    Open [dns.stackryze.com](https://dns.stackryze.com) and sign in with Stackryze SSO.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the zone">
    Click **Add Zone** and enter your domain name (for example `example.com`). Stackryze provisions the zone on PowerDNS in `pending_verification` status.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Point registrar nameservers to Stackryze">
    In your registrar's DNS or Nameservers settings, set custom nameservers to the three Stackryze authoritative hosts:

    ```text
    ns1.stackryze.com
    ns2.stackryze.com
    ns3.stackryze.com
    ```

    Save your registrar changes. Propagation usually starts within minutes, though long-tail resolver caches can take up to 48 hours.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify delegation in Stackryze DNS">
    Click **Verify** on your zone in [dns.stackryze.com](https://dns.stackryze.com). Stackryze runs an uncached `NS` lookup and activates the zone (`active`) as soon as at least one Stackryze nameserver appears in the live NS response.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test with dig">
    ```bash
    dig NS example.com @8.8.8.8 +short
    ```
  </Step>
</Steps>
