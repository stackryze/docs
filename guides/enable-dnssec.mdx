---
title: "Enable DNSSEC"
description: "Enable ECDSA P-256 CSK signing on your Stackryze DNS zone and publish the DS record at your registrar."
icon: "shield-check"
---

Stackryze DNS supports DNSSEC signing on PowerDNS zones using **ECDSA P-256 (`ECDSAP256SHA256`, Algorithm 13)**.

## Prerequisites

- An active, provisioned zone on [dns.stackryze.com](https://dns.stackryze.com) (platform subdomains must complete ownership verification first so the zone exists in PowerDNS).
- Access to publish `DS` records at your domain registrar.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open DNSSEC settings for your zone">
    Open your zone in [dns.stackryze.com](https://dns.stackryze.com) and go to its **DNSSEC** section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable signing">
    Click **Enable DNSSEC** (or send `POST /api/zones/:id/dnssec`). Stackryze creates an active CSK (`ECDSAP256SHA256`), rectifies the zone, and returns the computed `DS` record(s).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish the DS record at your registrar">
    Copy the `DS` record returned by Stackryze and add it in your domain registrar's DNSSEC panel so resolvers can validate the chain from the root zone down to your `DNSKEY`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify DNSSEC answers">
    ```bash
    dig +dnssec DNSKEY example.com @ns1.stackryze.com
    delv @1.1.1.1 example.com +rtrace +multiline
    ```

    `delv` prints `; fully validated` once the registrar publishes the `DS` record and TTLs expire.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Before clicking **Disable DNSSEC** (`DELETE /api/zones/:id/dnssec`), remove the `DS` record at your registrar first and wait for it to expire. Deleting cryptokeys while a parent `DS` still exists causes validating resolvers to return `SERVFAIL`.
</Warning>
