Stackryze DNS provides built-in DNSSEC signing powered by PowerDNS cryptokeys. Resolvers that validate DNSSEC can cryptographically verify that records in your zone originate from your authoritative zone and were not altered in transit.

## How Stackryze DNS signs zones

When you enable DNSSEC on an active zone (`POST /api/zones/:id/dnssec`):

1. PowerDNS creates an active **Combined Signing Key (`CSK`)** using **`ECDSAP256SHA256` (Algorithm 13)**.
2. Stackryze triggers zone rectification (`PUT /rectify`) so `RRSIG` and denial-of-existence records are generated immediately.
3. A PowerDNS `NOTIFY` pushes the signed zone to secondary nameservers.
4. The response returns your active `keys` array along with the formatted **`DS`** record strings to publish at your domain registrar.

## Enabling and disabling DNSSEC

<Steps>
  <Step title="Confirm your zone is provisioned">
    DNSSEC requires the zone to exist in PowerDNS. Complete ownership verification (for Stackryze subdomains) first so your zone is provisioned; otherwise enabling DNSSEC returns `409 Conflict`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable DNSSEC in the dashboard or API">
    Open your zone in [dns.stackryze.com](https://dns.stackryze.com) and enable DNSSEC (or call `POST /api/zones/:id/dnssec`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish the DS record at your registrar">
    Copy the returned `DS` record(s) into your registrar's DNSSEC panel so validating resolvers can link your parent zone to your `DNSKEY`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To remove signing and delete all cryptokeys on the zone, click **Disable DNSSEC** (or call `DELETE /api/zones/:id/dnssec`). Remove the `DS` record from your registrar first so validating resolvers do not reject unsigned answers with `SERVFAIL`.

## Verifying the chain

```bash
# Check DNSKEY and RRSIG answers directly from Stackryze
dig +dnssec DNSKEY example.com @ns1.stackryze.com

# Check DS publication and validation through a validating resolver
dig DS example.com @1.1.1.1
delv @1.1.1.1 example.com +rtrace +multiline
```
