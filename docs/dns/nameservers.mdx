Stackryze DNS serves hosted zones across three authoritative nameservers powered by PowerDNS Master/Slave replication.

## Authoritative nameservers

| Hostname | Region | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `ns1.stackryze.com` | New York, Americas | Primary / authoritative PowerDNS endpoint |
| `ns2.stackryze.com` | Frankfurt, Europe | Authoritative PowerDNS replica |
| `ns3.stackryze.com` | Hyderabad, Asia | Authoritative PowerDNS replica |

All three nameservers are assigned automatically when a zone is provisioned:

```text
ns1.stackryze.com
ns2.stackryze.com
ns3.stackryze.com
```

Zone mutations are written to the PowerDNS master and replicated to secondary nodes with `ALLOW-AXFR-FROM` restriction and `NOTIFY` propagation.

## How delegation verification works

When you click **Verify** (`POST /api/zones/:id/verify`), Stackryze DNS performs an uncached `NS` lookup to verify your domain actually delegates to Stackryze:

1. Attempts a live `dig` query against `8.8.8.8` (`+short +time=3 +tries=2`).
2. Falls back if needed to a multi-resolver uncached lookup pool covering **Google (`8.8.8.8`, `8.8.4.4`)**, **Cloudflare (`1.1.1.1`, `1.0.0.1`)**, **Quad9 (`9.9.9.9`)**, **OpenDNS (`208.67.222.222`)**, **DNS.Watch**, and **Comodo**.
3. Checks whether at least one of `ns1.stackryze.com`, `ns2.stackryze.com`, or `ns3.stackryze.com` is returned in the live `NS` record set.
4. Updates zone status to `active` once delegation is detected.

## Daily background re-verification

An automated verification job runs daily at `03:00` across all `active` zones:

- If your nameservers remain pointed at Stackryze DNS, `firstFailureAt` is cleared.
- If a previously `active` zone stops resolving to Stackryze nameservers (or starts failing with `NXDOMAIN` / `SERVFAIL`), a **7-day grace window** starts before transitioning the zone back to `pending_verification`.
- Transient network timeouts (`TIMEOUT` / `REFUSED`) do not penalize active zones.

## Verifying manually with `dig`

```bash
dig NS example.com @8.8.8.8 +short
```

Confirm direct answers from a Stackryze nameserver:

```bash
dig A example.com @ns1.stackryze.com +short
```
