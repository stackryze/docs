Stackryze Domains lets you claim public subdomains at no cost for open-source projects, portfolios, APIs, and self-hosted services at [domain.stackryze.com](https://domain.stackryze.com).

## Available extensions

You can register a single-label subdomain under any of five namespaces:

```text
yourname.indevs.in
yourname.sryze.cc
yourname.ryzedns.org
yourname.nx.kg
yourname.ryzn.pro
```

All five extensions share the same validation rules, nameserver delegation options, WHOIS support, and 1-year lifecycle.

## What is included

- **Default quota of 5 domains** — 1 slot per extension (`.indevs.in`, `.sryze.cc`, `.ryzedns.org`, `.nx.kg`, and `.ryzn.pro`), increasable by request via [Discord](https://discord.gg/wr7s97cfM7) or [support@stackryze.com](mailto:support@stackryze.com).
- **Full BYOD delegation** — defaults to `ns1.stackryze.com`, `ns2.stackryze.com`, and `ns3.stackryze.com`, or point to 2 or more custom nameservers (Cloudflare, Route 53, DigitalOcean, Stackryze DNS, or self-hosted PowerDNS).
- **1-year validity with free annual renewal** — renewable starting 60 days before expiry through a 15-day post-expiry grace period.
- **Public WHOIS with privacy protection** — live lookups at [domain.stackryze.com/whois](https://domain.stackryze.com/whois) with personal name, address, phone, and (by default) email redacted.

<Note>
  GitHub verification (starring [stackryze/FreeDomains](https://github.com/stackryze/FreeDomains) with a GitHub account at least 30 days old) is required before registering a subdomain on any extension. See [Account & Domain Verification](/account-verification).
</Note>

## How it fits with Stackryze DNS

| Product | Role |
| --- | --- |
| **Stackryze Domains** (`domain.stackryze.com`) | Parent-zone delegation registry. Creates and updates `NS` records for your subdomain and tracks expiry, renewals, and WHOIS. |
| **Stackryze DNS** (`dns.stackryze.com`) | Optional authoritative record manager (`A`, `AAAA`, `CNAME`, `MX`, `TXT`, `SRV`, `CAA`, DNSSEC, API tokens). Or use any third-party DNS provider. |

## Scope and boundaries

- Sibling subdomains are isolated for DNS delegation, and parent zones are operated as shared public community namespaces.
- A registered name cannot be transferred between accounts or moved between extensions through the dashboard.
- Nameservers are updated directly in our authoritative PowerDNS parent zones (`TTL 3600`); sub-records (`A`, `CNAME`, `TXT`, etc.) must be configured on your chosen DNS provider (or on [dns.stackryze.com](https://dns.stackryze.com)).
