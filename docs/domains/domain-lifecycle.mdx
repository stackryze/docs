Every Stackryze Domain is registered for **1 year** and follows an automated daily lifecycle schedule run at `00:00` UTC.

## Domain statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Active** | Registered, delegated in PowerDNS, and resolving normally. |
| **Expired** | Past its expiry timestamp (Days 0–15 post-expiry). Grace period is active; delegation stays live and you can still renew. |
| **Pending Deletion** | User submitted a deletion request. Domain stays live until reviewed by an administrator. |
| **Suspended** | Disabled by an administrator due to abuse or policy violation. DNS delegation is removed and modifications/renewals are blocked. |
| **Soft-Deleted (Cooling-Off)** | Exceeded the 15-day grace period (or an approved deletion). PowerDNS delegation is removed, quota is freed, and the name is locked for 7 days. |

## Annual timeline

Measured relative to a domain's expiry date:

| Timing | Event |
| --- | --- |
| **Day 0 (Creation)** | Subdomain is registered for 1 year (`Active`) and PowerDNS parent delegation (`NS`, `TTL 3600`) is written immediately. |
| **60 days before expiry** | Free **Renewal window opens** and the first expiry reminder email is sent. |
| **10 days before expiry** | Final pre-expiry reminder email is sent. |
| **Expiry Date (Day 0)** | Unrenewed domains automatically transition from `Active` to `Expired`. DNS delegation remains intact. |
| **Days 1–15 after expiry** | **15-day grace period.** Domain still resolves and can still be renewed in the dashboard (renewing resets status back to `Active`). |
| **Day 15+ after expiry** | **Auto-soft-delete.** Daily cron deletes PowerDNS delegation, decrements your active extension quota, notifies you by email, and starts a **7-day cooling-off period**. |
| **Day 22+ after expiry** | **Permanent removal.** Soft-deleted record is purged after 7 days in cooling-off and the label becomes available for anyone to register again. |

## Renewing a domain

Renewals are free and extend `expiresAt` by **1 full year**:

- Available from **60 days before expiry** until the end of the **15-day post-expiry grace period**.
- Blocked for domains in **`Suspended`** or **`Pending Deletion`** state.
- Renewing an `Expired` domain inside the 15-day grace period automatically restores its status to `Active`.
- If you renew while time still remains, 1 year is added from the current `expiresAt` date (or from the current time if already expired).

## Requesting deletion

Submitting **Delete Domain** in the **Danger Zone** does **not** immediately erase your DNS records:

1. Your subdomain moves to **`Pending Deletion`** so it cannot be edited or renewed while queued.
2. You receive a confirmation email and the request waits for administrator review.
3. Your subdomain continues resolving until an administrator approves the deletion.
4. Once deleted, the name enters the **7-day cooling-off period** before it can be registered again.

<Warning>
  Once a domain has exceeded the 15-day post-expiry grace period and is auto-soft-deleted, its PowerDNS delegation is removed and the label enters cooling-off. Keep your contact email up to date in **Settings** so renewal reminders reach your inbox.
</Warning>
