Stackryze ships open, developer-focused domain and DNS infrastructure backed by PowerDNS, centralized single sign-on, and modern web APIs.

Whether you are a founder, freelancer, small team, or just curious, you can register free public subdomains on **Stackryze Domains** and manage authoritative DNS zones on **Stackryze DNS**.

<Note>
  Stackryze **Domains** is live at [domain.stackryze.com](https://domain.stackryze.com) and Stackryze **DNS** is live at [dns.stackryze.com](https://dns.stackryze.com). All accounts sign in through Stackryze SSO at [auth.stackryze.com](https://auth.stackryze.com).
</Note>

## Architecture overview

The Stackryze network splits naming and resolution into two cooperating services:

| Product | URL | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Stackryze Domains** | [domain.stackryze.com](https://domain.stackryze.com) | Free public subdomains across five extensions (`.indevs.in`, `.sryze.cc`, `.ryzedns.org`, `.nx.kg`, `.ryzn.pro`), annual renewals, WHOIS, and NS delegation. |
| **Stackryze DNS** | [dns.stackryze.com](https://dns.stackryze.com) | Managed authoritative PowerDNS hosting for custom domains or verified Stackryze subdomains, with REST API tokens, DNSSEC, scheduled changes, and webhooks. |

## Official communication channels

Stackryze only sends official mail and support replies from `@stackryze.com`:

- `support@stackryze.com` — account help, quota increases, and general support
- `reportabuse@stackryze.com` — phishing, malware, and Acceptable Use Policy reports
- `security@stackryze.com` — vulnerability reports and security incidents
- `no-reply@stackryze.com` — automated verification, renewal, and lifecycle notices
