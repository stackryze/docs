Stackryze separates authentication, anti-abuse identity verification, and DNS control across three clear checks. No payment method or card authorization is required anywhere on Stackryze.

## Verification layers overview

| Check | Where it applies | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Stackryze SSO** | Both [domain.stackryze.com](https://domain.stackryze.com) and [dns.stackryze.com](https://dns.stackryze.com) | Centralized sign-in, Passkeys, and 2FA via [auth.stackryze.com](https://auth.stackryze.com) (OIDC). |
| **GitHub Star KYC** | Stackryze Domains (`domain.stackryze.com`) | Confirms a real developer account (at least 30 days old) before claiming subdomains on any of the five extensions. |
| **NS & Ownership Verification** | Stackryze DNS (`dns.stackryze.com`) | Confirms your domain delegates to Stackryze nameservers (`ns1–ns3.stackryze.com`) and links platform subdomains via a one-time code. |

## 1. Stackryze SSO (`auth.stackryze.com`)

Both platforms authenticate users through our central OIDC identity provider at [auth.stackryze.com](https://auth.stackryze.com). Back-channel logout and session introspection keep your session state synchronized across services.

<Note>
  If your linked sign-in resolves to a `@users.noreply.github.com` address, Stackryze Domains prompts you to set and verify a personal email address before accessing the dashboard so you receive renewal and expiry alerts.
</Note>

## 2. GitHub KYC on Stackryze Domains

To prevent automated spam and abuse across our shared domain extensions, every account must complete GitHub verification once before registering subdomains under `.indevs.in`, `.sryze.cc`, `.ryzedns.org`, `.nx.kg`, or `.ryzn.pro`.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Star the repository">
    Open [github.com/stackryze/FreeDomains](https://github.com/stackryze/FreeDomains) while signed into your GitHub account and click **Star**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start verification in the dashboard">
    Go to **Register Domain** or **My Domains** on [domain.stackryze.com](https://domain.stackryze.com) and click **Verify with GitHub**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize via GitHub OAuth">
    Stackryze checks three requirements automatically:
    - Your GitHub account is at least **30 days old**
    - That GitHub account ID is not already linked to another Stackryze account
    - Your GitHub account has starred `stackryze/FreeDomains`
  </Step>
</Steps>

Once verified, you unlock registration quotas across all five public extensions (1 domain per extension by default).

## 3. Zone verification on Stackryze DNS

Stackryze DNS includes **3 zones per account** and **200 records per zone** by default, along with API tokens (`sk_dns_...`), webhooks, DNSSEC (`ECDSAP256SHA256`), temporary records, and scheduled changes—no extra verification fee is required.

To keep zones healthy:

- **External domains** (`example.com`) start in `pending_verification`. Point your registrar's NS records to `ns1.stackryze.com`, `ns2.stackryze.com`, and `ns3.stackryze.com`, then click **Verify** (`POST /api/zones/:id/verify`). Our daily verifier checks active zones and allows a **7-day grace period** if nameservers change unexpectedly.
- **Stackryze subdomains** (`yourname.indevs.in`, `.sryze.cc`, `.ryzedns.org`, `.nx.kg`, `.ryzn.pro`) start in `pending_ownership`. Paste the generated `sryze-verify-...` code into your subdomain's **DNS Ownership Verification** panel on [domain.stackryze.com](https://domain.stackryze.com), click **Verify Ownership** on Stackryze DNS (`POST /api/zones/:id/verify-ownership`), and then complete the NS check.
